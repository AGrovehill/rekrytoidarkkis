Profile

My background is in modelling and simulating complex energy systems: CFD, thermal analysis, and process behaviour, with turbomachinery among the domains I've worked in. Most of my work lives in the overlap: fluid flow, heat transfer, process engineering, simulation, R&D, and optimisation, plus whatever custom tool gets the job done faster. Comfortable in Linux and HPC environments.

What I do

  • Setting up and running CFD simulations from start to finish: meshing, boundary conditions, results
  • Planning test campaigns: what to measure, how to instrument it, and what the data actually needs to tell us
  • Digging through test and simulation data to see whether the design does what it's supposed to
  • Building process models so we get a picture of how a system behaves before it's even built
  • Writing Python tools once doing the same thing by hand for the fifth time stops making sense
  • Working with mechanical, electrical, and test teams to keep gas turbine R&D moving
  • Writing things down so the next person doesn't have to redo the work

What I'm looking for

Three years into my current job, I've become the person people come to for CFD, testing, process modelling, and tooling, often all at once. I've picked up a fair number of different roles along the way, and honestly, I'd like to put a few of them down. What I want now is more focus: room to take one hard problem all the way to a solid result, instead of juggling ten things at once.

I also want my work to point somewhere I actually believe in. That's why I got into energy engineering in the first place: I wanted a career that moves things in the right direction. I'm especially interested in renewable energy, emissions reduction, and efficiency work — the kind of thing that's a footnote in a lot of job ads but would actually be the job here.

I'm open to relocating within Finland if the job's worth it. I'm not looking for just any next step: I want something genuinely interesting, technically deep, and worth doing properly.
